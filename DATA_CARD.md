# Data Card

GridRescue AI uses three kinds of data: **open data read live** from public services, **small bundled files** for offline use and defaults, and **synthetic sites** generated to train the classifier. No personal, patient or customer data is used or stored.

## Motivation

The planner must work for any map location without API keys, so it reads global open data and keeps small snapshots for offline use. The classifier needs many labelled examples, and no public dataset labels sites with the best backup architecture, so the engine labels synthetic sites itself.

### Open data read at run time (not redistributed)

NASA POWER (climatology), PVGIS (EU JRC), NLR PVWatts, Global Solar Atlas, Open-Meteo (forecast, ERA5, GloFAS, elevation, geocoding; CC BY 4.0), OpenStreetMap Overpass and Nominatim, World Bank Indicators, ENERGYDATA.INFO. Responses are cached on the user's machine in `backend/.cache/`, which is git-ignored.

### Synthetic training sites

10,000 sites drawn by `backend/app/ml/train_backup_classifier.py` (seed 2026): site resources, grid class, facility kind and size, backup hours, roof and land area, existing generator and diesel price. Each is labelled with the family of the engine's recommended architecture. The set is **simulated, not real**, and is regenerated on demand rather than stored.

## Collection and Generation

Bundled snapshots were built with `backend/tools/build_osm_snapshot.py` (OpenStreetMap) or compiled by hand from the cited publications. Synthetic sites are sampled from the distributions in the training script.

## Schema and Units

- Coordinates in decimal degrees (WGS 84); power in kW, energy in kWh, solar in peak-sun hours (kWh/m² per day), wind in m/s at 50 m, prices in local currency per kWh or per litre, with US$ conversions at the stated exchange rate.
- Tariff entries carry `source` fields; hospital entries carry `beds`, `tier` and coordinates; state entries carry `disco` and `class`.

## Preprocessing

OpenStreetMap features are filtered to power, health and water tags and simplified to points and polylines. Synthetic sites are turned into the 21 classifier features by `features_from()`.

## Splitting Strategy

Synthetic sites: first 80 % (8,000) for training, last 20 % (2,000) held out for testing, from one seeded sequence.

## Known Biases

- Grid classes and resources are weighted toward Nigerian and sub-Saharan conditions.
- Hospital coordinates in the curated lists are approximate; live OpenStreetMap geometry replaces them when online.
- OpenStreetMap coverage of substations and lines varies by region and is incomplete in places.

## Limitations

- The snapshot reflects OpenStreetMap at extraction time and is not updated automatically.
- Tariffs and diesel prices change; each carries the date of its source in its citation.
- The state reliability classes are defaults; the hospital's reported supply hours override them.

## Privacy

No personal data is collected or bundled. Hospital entries are public institutions. The assessment history holds site and design summaries only, in a local git-ignored file.

## Maintenance

Maintained by the authors. Report data errors with the **Bug report** issue form, citing a better source.

## Intended Use

Pre-feasibility planning and research on backup power for critical facilities. Not for grid operations or safety-critical control.
